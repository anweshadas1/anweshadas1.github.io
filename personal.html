Writing

I have been writing for what feels like ever and always, as much as my sporadic hyperfixations (🌻) allow, that is. Somewhere on the internet, at addresses postfixed with a long-archaic (perhaps dying) .blogspot.com and a not-so-long-archaic .tumblr.com, there is more than a decade of writing by a considerably younger person with a flair for theatrics, shameful amounts of self-deprecation and shockingly firm opinions about books. I am going back through it and reposting whatever survives a second reading, original dates attached.
